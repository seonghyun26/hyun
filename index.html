Bio

Hello! I'm a 2nd year Ph.D. student at KAIST AI, advised by Sungsoo Ahn in the Lab. My research interests are mainly in AI4Science, integrating machine learning to advance scientific discovery on small molecules and proteins! I'm interested in the following topics:

  • Drug design and molecular interactions
  • Protein dynamics and Collective Variables (CVs)
  • Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), Over-squashing
In the long term, I envision building an AI-native scientific discovery platform with autonomous agents on molecules and proteins, enabling end-to-end self-improving scientific workflows!!
